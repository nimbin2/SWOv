swov: enter follows the keyboard, and the cursor waits to be asked

Two faults with one cause: the selection could be set by a pointer that merely
happened to be resting somewhere, and nothing told that apart from a choice
someone made. Two flags now do.

sel_by_key — the keyboard put the selection there. Tab and the arrows pick
exactly one thing, so enter acts on that and nothing else: a workspace when
that is what was picked, which is how tabbing through hits and pressing enter
is supposed to work. The previous release made enter re-ask the query whenever
the selection was not itself a matching window, to stop a resting pointer from
stealing the hit; tabbing to a workspace leaves no window picked and tripped
exactly that condition, so enter threw away where you had tabbed to and jumped
back to the first hit. Only an incidental selection goes back to the query now.

mouse_moved — the pointer has actually moved since the window opened. Until
then it selects nothing and no cursor is drawn, so opening the overview no
longer lights up whatever the mouse was left on top of. A key, a click, a
relayed pointer from swas, or a query showing its hit all bring the cursor out;
start_selection still decides where the keyboard starts from.

Hover had to stop keying off "what is under the pointer changed" for this. A
new window is sent a motion event for wherever the pointer already is, so that
position was recorded before movement counted, and moving inside the same tile
afterwards changed nothing to notice — the cursor only appeared once the
pointer crossed into a different workspace. Found by the test below, not by
reading it.

Checked against the real decision with a stand-in sway on a socketpair, ten
cases: enter after typing a query, after tabbing once, twice and three times,
onto an empty workspace, onto a workspace matched only by name, with a pointer
resting somewhere nobody searched, with no query at all, and the cursor's
visibility across a fresh open, a first motion event, a 1px jitter, a real
move and a tab.
