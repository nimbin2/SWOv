swov: fix enter after a search, clicking a free number, and cropped SVG icons

Enter during a search opened whatever the cursor happened to be on. Pointing
at anything selects it, so a pointer resting elsewhere — or merely passed over
on the way to the keyboard — replaced the hit the query had chosen, and enter
focused a workspace nobody searched for. Enter now checks that what is
selected is itself a match and asks the query again if it is not, falling back
to a workspace whose own name matched, and flashing the field when nothing
did. The best-hit pick moved out of apply_filter so it can be asked twice.

Clicking a free number did nothing because it quit instead. The hit test only
knows the workspaces sway actually has, so a free slot looked like a click on
the empty background, and the press handler closed the overview before the
release could act on it. A press on a slot now arms the click like any other.

An SVG icon whose drawing runs outside its declared width/height was cut off:
SDL_image rasterises to the box the file declares and simply does not draw
past it. nanosvg is vendored here now (the same copy swas uses) and the box is
the declared canvas and the shapes' own bounds together — an icon with
deliberate padding keeps it, one that overflows is scaled down instead of
losing an edge. SDL_image stays as the fallback.

Checked against a 48x48 icon whose art reaches 88x88: ink running into the
bottom and right edges drops from 54 pixels to 9, and well-formed icons
rasterise byte for byte as before.
