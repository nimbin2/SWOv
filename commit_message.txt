sw_draw: size a font by its em square, not by ascent-to-descent

sw_font(px) asked stb_truetype for ScaleForPixelHeight, which makes the
ascent-to-descent span px pixels tall. Every other toolkit — FreeType's
set_pixel_sizes, SDL_ttf's TTF_OpenFont, CSS — means the em square by a font
size. The gap is the font's line height over its em, 1.21 for Inter and
about 1.16 for DejaVu, so text came out roughly a fifth smaller than the
number asked for. It is ScaleForMappingEmToPixels now.

Note for swbr, which still has its own copy of this code: rebuilding it
against this header makes its text that much larger, so its px values want
lowering by about a sixth to keep the bar looking the way it does.
